# Owen's answers (Quest Log, October 2026)

Owen answered all fifty Quest Log questions online. His words, unedited except for "John" read as Jon. What got built from each is in brackets.

## Rune 1: After you play
1. Best part: Jon really made me laugh because all of his snarky comments are funny.
2. Died a lot fighting the penguin boss and a lot fighting those little flying coins things. [Penguin King 10 → 8 health, longer daze, fewer icicles. The "flying coins" are read as the wisps: slower, and a wisp now pops when it hits you.]
3. Tough in some spots, easier/simpler in others. [Bosses stay tough; swarm damage softened.]
4. More influence from Led Zeppelin or Queen; otherwise the music is fine. [Stairway to Valhalla (Led Zeppelin sound) for the Forest Giant fight; We Will Chop You (Queen sound) for the Penguin King.]
5. All of Jon's comments were regular Jon, like I imagined.
6. iPad buttons in the right places; it all felt good.
7. It is missing one band (Led Zeppelin); liked the Queen song and the Aerosmith song.
8. Not a fan of the spitting mushroom; the rest are fun. Replace it with a werewolf-style thing. [The Varg: prowls, howls, lunges, then pants. Faster at night.]
9. The plane dream should go and just be a one-timer. [Nothing in the game points back to it anymore. The nightmare dungeon is still in the code; say the word and it's removed entirely.]
10. Jon should sing a Led Zeppelin song badly, to raise the mood. [Done, plus Rush E.]

## Rune 2: Jon
11. Jon says things like "you're terrible" and "do better," but with a positive influence. [In his hurt, kill and idle lines.]
12. Jon is based on a book Owen read: a flying, talking sword who speaks a lot of Spanish. [Spanish throughout.]
13. Running joke: Jon is terrible at singing Rush E or Led Zeppelin. [Done.]
14. Scared of spiders; brags about being a magical talking flying axe. [Spiders in Fluffy's tunnel; brag lines.]
15. Nicest thing: Jon compliments you, worth half a heart, so you can keep fighting. [Jon's Compliment: at one heart, once every 25 seconds.]
16. New power: summon fire from the Nordic underworld, an instant shot. [Fire of Helheim, as an Arena card for now.]
17. Meeting Walter: "Yay, my creator!" and then he gets sick of him.
18. Jon stays the whole story. He gets confiscated at one point, and Owen goes to a giants' palace to reclaim him.
19. A charm only Jon would want: a mango. [Lars sells it; Jon swings faster.]
20. Jon hates cucumbers and squash. [Lines.]

22. Chilly is a vampire-style guy; in daylight his skin falls off. He's also a penguin, and penguins don't like being hot. [Tablet.]
23. Hank threw the axe in the lake to keep it away from people like Chilly.
24. Fluffy is just a random dog.
25. Jon is the one who says Walter's real name (Pickles) first.
26. Nobody says Walter's name because names have power in Nordic stories. [Tablet.]
27. Forest Giants can summon leaves to wrap up opponents, Demeter-style, and can heal people (they don't). [The giant's leaf wrap.]
28. The giant turns back into a log when he loses. [He does, and snores beside the path.]
29. The penguins have penguin sense instead of spider sense. [Jon line.]
30. "Jjorvic" because it sounded Nordic.
