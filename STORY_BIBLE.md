# Jöntuka: A Viking Tale — Story Bible (draft)

Owen's journal pages are canon and override everything here. Everything else is Claude's draft for Owen to rewrite, rename or veto. Names marked *(placeholder)* need Owen's call.

## The premise

A bizillion years ago, Walter the Wonderful forged Jöntuka in a volcano. Hank, a Viking chieftain, threw the axe into a Norwegian lake so only someone with Viking blood could pull it out. He was hiding it from **Chilly the Frost Demon**. Chilly wants Jöntuka because it is the one blade that can crack open the volcano's heart and let winter swallow the world.

Owen fell into that lake while running from a possessed penguin, and Jon found him. The penguin killed Owen. Jon's voodoo magic bonded them for eternity and brought Owen back as an undead voodoo magic zombie. Now Jon wants to go back to Norway: unfinished business.

**The joke that holds the world together:** penguins don't live in Norway. That's how everyone knows they're possessed.

## Chapter 2 as Owen wrote it (page 5, built)

The flight attendant was "perfectly normal (it was a dream)", so the Red-Eye is now a nightmare Owen can choose to have later. She points Owen to the subway station south-west of the airstrip. In the Fjordvik T-bane he works the exchangy doohickey for 200 kroner, then pays a taxi 20 kr to the forest (walking is still allowed). The old Whispering Forest is now the **Muck Forest**: muck slows Owen and splashes, tall grass rustles and hides drops, and a fallen log blocks the way east. When Owen reaches it, Jon flies out: "Hello Señor! Ooh, that's a big log, want me to chop it?" "Jon..." Three swings clear the log for good and the Frozen Path leads to the **Penguin Palace** (renamed from the Ice Cavern; it has a throne behind the King). Beating it shows CHAPTER 2 COMPLETE.

## Page 7: the blur, the time-out signal, the giants' war (built)

Owen's page plays inside the Forest Giant fight in three beats, in his words. A few seconds in: the blur ("The giant swings, I dodge, I swing, I miss"), Jon sings, Owen tells him to be quiet. At half health Owen is tired and accidentally makes the time-out signal, a T, which giants take as a sign of war: the giant gets faster for the rest of the fight. As the giant hides, Owen remembers the giants' war and gathers his wits; the page's cliffhanger is kept, and the fight continues.

**Giant lore (canon):** the four giant clans are North, South, East and West. North despises South; East despises West. In their war North sided with East and South with West. Ever since, East and North like each other. A rune tablet in the foothills now carries this (replacing the smith's-name tease; the forge tablet still says names have power).

**For Owen's next page:** how does Owen use the giants' war to win? Claim to be from the East? Call the South giants?

## Page 6: the Forest Giant of the North (built)

Updated from Owen's answers: every third attack the giant wraps a ring of ground in leaves (dash or step out); when he loses he turns back into a log that rolls off the path and snores; the Leaf Rune (the fifth rune) appears in the Muck Forest afterward; the fight plays the Led Zeppelin-sound piece.

The third chop on the log wakes it. Owen's page plays word for word: "Who awakens the Forest Giant of the NORTH?!" / "Um, we were looking for Jjorvic Palace." / "Ah, the penguins told me about you." / Jon: "Señor, can we get to the big boss fight scene please?" / "Later, Jon." The giant raises his club, and the fight starts where the page ends.

The fight: he is fifty feet tall, so chops bounce off ("TOO TALL"). He stalks, raises the club (a shadow tracks Owen, then commits), slams it for two hearts, and the club sticks in the muck: that is the window to chop his ankles. Fire burns him any time (giants hate fire). Below half health he hides as a tree: three trees sprout, one of them can't hold still; throw Jon at it to find him stuck. When he falls, the log is gone for good and the Frozen Path opens. A runestone stands in the forest for retries. The palace is **Jjorvic Palace** (Owen's name) everywhere.

## The SNES pass (built)

What the classics do that the game now does too: a day/night cycle with dusk and dawn tints, stars and fireflies (20 real minutes per day); drifting cloud shadows; snow on the frozen screens; damage numbers in story mode (*Secret of Mana*); a MAP OF NORWAY page in the gear menu that fills in as you explore and tracks hearts, tablets, side quests and chapters; eight hidden **rune tablets** with lore (Hank, Walter's name, Chilly's weakness) that grant a heart container; and three enemies with distinct rules: the **Spore Cap** (spits from range, hides under its cap up close so swings bounce), the **Slime** (hops; big ones split into two minis) and the **Beetle** (armored; a thrown Jon flips it, then chop the belly before it rights itself).

## The Red-Eye (optional, genuinely creepy)

You don't have to do it. After the Penguin Palace you can sit on the bench by the plane and fall asleep; the dream puts you in the cargo hold. Inside:

- **Darkness.** You can only see in a small circle around Jon's glow, and the lights flicker.
- **Scratching that follows you.** You hear it move between rooms before you see anything.
- **Seat rows that rearrange** when you're not looking.
- **The attendant's refreshment cart** rolls down the aisle on its own and has to be dodged.
- **Boss:** the attendant in her full form, fast and teleporting, who only shows on screen in the flicker frames. It ends with "Any refreshments, sir?"
- **Limits:** no gore and no blood. It's scary through sound, darkness and things that move wrong.

## Items: the Bag and Charms

The BAG page holds food and charms. Smoked Salmon (Lars, 15 kr, or dropped by enemies) heals two hearts; Lutefisk (8 kr) heals everything and Jon will never let it go; Rune Shards fill the meter. ## Arena mode: "Valhalla Arena" (*Brotato*-style, built in milestone 2)

- **Waves:** 20 timed waves of mixed enemies (penguins, draugr, wisps, bats, knights, then elites). Every fifth wave is a boss.
- **Between waves:** a shop offers random weapon and skill cards paid for with kroner dropped in the arena. Examples: a second Jon, frost aura, chain lightning, a lingonberry regen tick, a bigger swing arc, and penguin repellent.
- **Builds stack:** cards combine, for example Homing plus Chain Lightning, or Voodoo Dash plus dash damage.
- **Unlocks:** the arena unlocks after the Penguin Palace. Story items show up as rare arena cards.
