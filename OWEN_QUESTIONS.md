# Questions for Owen

Prompts to move the story and the game forward. Ask a few at a time, ideally right after he plays. Write his answers in here (or have him write them) and they become the next build.

Rules of thumb: his journal pages always win over anything already in the game. Short answers are fine. "I don't know yet" is fine too; skip it and come back.

## After he plays (10 minutes, watch, don't help)

1. What was the best part? What made you laugh?
2. Where did you get stuck or confused? Where did you die?
3. Was anything too easy? Too hard? (The Penguin King? The draugr shields? Hank?)
4. Which song did you like best? Any song that got annoying?
5. Did Jon say anything funny? Anything that didn't sound like him?
6. On the iPad: were the buttons in the right places? Did the stick feel right?

## Jon (based on the real Jon)

7. What does the real Jon say all the time? Give me three catchphrases.
8. What's a running joke between you two? Can it go in the game?
9. What would Jon be scared of? What would he brag about?
10. What's the nicest thing Jon would do for you? (That becomes his top ability.)
11. If Jon could learn one more power, what would it be?

## The story so far (fill the gaps the game had to guess at)

12. What happens right after "I tried to call Jon" on the plane? Does Jon bust out of the cargo hold?
13. Who is Chilly, really? What does he look like? Why does he want Jöntuka?
14. Why can't Chilly walk in daylight?
15. What does Hank look like, and why did he throw the axe in the lake?
16. Was Fluffy Hank's dog? What kind of dog? (If not, who is Fluffy?)
17. Walter the Wonderful's real name is Pickles. How does everyone find out? Who says it first?
18. Why doesn't anyone want to say Walter's real name?

## Chapter 3: Walter's Forge (the volcano)

19. What's inside the volcano? Lava rivers? Rooms? A giant anvil?
20. What's the boss? (A forge golem? A fire penguin? Walter's robot?)
21. What new power does Jon get in the forge? (Right now the plan is a Rune Shield where Jon spins around you and blocks.)
22. Is Walter (Pickles) alive, a ghost, or just his tools and notes?
23. What does Jon feel about meeting the guy who made him?

## Chapter 4 and the ending

24. What's on top of the Storm Peak?
25. How do you beat Chilly? What's his weakness?
26. Does Owen stay a zombie at the end, or get fixed? Does he want to?
27. What happens to Jon at the end? Does he stay with Owen?
28. What's the very last line of the story?

## New enemies and items (pick any)

29. Name three more possessed animals that don't belong in Norway.
30. Invent an enemy that's funny AND dangerous.
31. What should be in the shop that isn't there yet?
32. What's a secret you'd love to find hidden in the world?
33. Design one heart-piece puzzle: where is it hidden and how do you get it?

## Valhalla Arena

34. Name three Arena upgrade cards. What do they do?
35. What should the final wave (wave 20) look like?
36. Should there be a leaderboard? Who are you trying to beat?

## Rush

37. Jon can recite Rush E. Should the real "Rush E" vibe show up more? Where?
38. Bjarne's song is called Rush Ø. What's Bjarne's next song called?

## Your page 5 (the subway, the taxi, the log) is in the game

41. The flight attendant was a dream. Do you still want the scary plane dream in the game as an optional nightmare, or should it go?
42. What does the Penguin Palace look like inside? Ice? Fish? A throne? What does the King say when you walk in?
43. After Jon chops the log, what happens next? Does the forest fight back?
44. The game turned the muck into slow mud and added tall grass. Right or wrong?
45. New enemies: a spitting mushroom, a slime that splits, and an armored beetle Jon flips. Which one is from your story and which should go?
46. Hidden rune tablets tell secrets about Hank, Walter and Chilly. What secret should one of them say?
